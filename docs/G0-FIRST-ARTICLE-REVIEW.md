# G0 first-article review sheet

Audience: student team and faculty advisor. Type: proposed review packet. Prepared: 2026-09-25. Decision status: open.

## Decision to make

Choose one primary first demonstration and the evidence it must produce. The recommended **first flight goal for review** is recovery of a conventional rocket with interpretable measured data. An inert folding-wing article can test the original transformation on a bench; a separate glider can test aircraft descent independently. These articles do not establish that the original integrated vehicle is feasible. The original concept remains in [user vision](../USER-VISION.md).

If the team instead wants a drone as the first flight project, complete [D0](DRONE-TRACK.md) for that independent track. Do not combine both aircraft into one first mission by default.

## Complete at the review

| Field | Answer to record |
|---|---|
| Primary demonstration and why | Open: select measured rocket recovery, inert wing behavior, independent glider, or another defined result |
| First article and separate follow-on articles | Open: identify C0, C1, C2, or a justified alternative; keep integration at G3 |
| Budget ceiling and reserve | Open: team-approved amount and what it includes |
| Owner and advisor/reviewer | Open: named people who accept their roles |
| Facilities and test-access route | Open: documented workshop and relevant range/aircraft-site contacts and permitted scope |
| Observable success and failure criteria | Open: define recoverability, usable measurements, inspection record, and any applicable limits before testing |
| Schedule constraint | Open: target review date and available team time, not a promised flight date |

Compare the selected mission with at least one alternative on evidence value, cost, access, recovery burden, and the skills it teaches. Record unknowns and who will resolve them. Do not treat the nominal 7–8 ft silhouette, Mach 1, a carried drone, or a rendered animation as a first-article requirement.

## Exit evidence

A dated disposition from the responsible team and advisor names the selected mission, owner, budget, reviewer, access route, and criteria. Link it from [D03 in the register](REGISTERS.md) and update [STATE.md](../STATE.md) and [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md). If any field remains unresolved, G0 stays open. This packet does not approve hardware orders, construction, launch, or aircraft operations.

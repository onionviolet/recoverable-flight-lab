# Propulsion, recovery, and presentation choices

Audience: team and advisor. Type: current state. Updated: 2026-09-08.

Recommendations here are proposed, not accepted purchases or flight requirements.

## Getting a working engine

For the conventional rocket track, obtain a commercially manufactured certified rocket motor through the qualified mentor and launch organization. Establish operator eligibility, launch scope, and vehicle requirements before selecting a motor. Check the current certified listing and supplier documentation. No engine purchase has been made or authorized by this brief. [NAR certification](https://www.nar.org/HPRCertification), [motor listing](https://www.nar.org/content.aspx?club_id=114127&module_id=669684&page_id=22)

The acquisition sequence is mentor/site route, reviewed conventional vehicle, compatible certified propulsion selected by the responsible team, then documented supplier procurement. This is procurement planning, not motor assembly or firing instructions.

The original fuel cylinder is not required as a separate fuel tank for a conventional commercial solid motor. That volume could serve payload and recovery functions after the architecture is reviewed.

## Would a jet engine be better?

| Option | Mission fit | Consequences | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certified rocket motor | Short powered ascent followed by recovery | Established student rocketry route; selection still depends on vehicle and range | Proposed rocket baseline |
| Purchased model turbine | Sustained powered aircraft flight | Requires air intake, fuel system, controls, installation review, heat management, maintenance | Separate aircraft project if sustained jet flight becomes the goal |
| Electric aircraft propulsion | Independent aircraft demonstrator | Battery and propulsor requirements; supports aircraft development | Consider for the aircraft track if powered flight is desired |
| Custom rocket or turbine engine | Propulsion research as the main mission | Dedicated facilities, expertise, testing, and budget dominate scope | Outside this project baseline |

A turbine uses atmospheric air; a rocket carries its oxidizer. A jet engine is therefore a different architecture, not an automatic rocket upgrade. [NASA propulsion](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/propulsion-system/)

Commercial model turbines exist, including JetCat products. The manufacturer provides model-specific documentation. This establishes availability, not suitability for this concept. No turbine model, price, or thrust requirement is selected. [JetCat manuals](https://www.jetcat.de/de/service/Bedienungsanleitungen)

For visual effect, the original turbine-inspired shape can be shown in an inert display. Flight hardware geometry must follow the selected propulsion and aerodynamic needs. Do not add a cosmetic nozzle or intake to flight hardware without technical review.

## Recovery options

| Option | What it offers | Added burden |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional parachute and tracker | Reference recovery route | Drift, deployment reliability, recovery access |
| Separate search drone | Helps locate landed hardware where permitted | Another operator and aircraft operation; does not replace tracking |
| Unpowered recovery glider | Wing deployment and aircraft learning | Lift, stability, wind, landing space, independent validation |
| Carried powered drone | Potential controlled aircraft recovery | Launch survival, packaging, release, power, transition, and additional approvals |
| Separate recovery for multiple modules | Preserves modular concept | Every piece adds a descent and recovery case |

U.S. amateur rocket and educational aircraft operations have distinct frameworks. Confirm the actual activity with the responsible operators. University status alone is not flight approval. [FAA rockets](https://www.faa.gov/space/licenses/amateur-rockets), [FAA educational users](https://www.faa.gov/uas/educational_users)

## Visual impressiveness without changing the flight mission

Build a full-size inert display if the silhouette matters. Use a removable side panel to show the mechanism. Demonstrate wing movement on a guarded stand. Show flight video with measured telemetry after a validated flight. Present a clearly labeled simulation beside the actual test data.

The showcase can communicate the long-term vision while the flight article remains smaller. Mark concept, bench demonstration, simulated result, and measured result directly on each exhibit.

## Speed versus reuse

Speed remains an optional future specialization. It adds aerodynamic and structural demands and can conflict with lightweight deployable recovery. Select it only through a separate mission trade study. The proposed first success is repeatable recovery with useful measurements.

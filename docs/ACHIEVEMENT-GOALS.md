# Tentative achievement goals

Audience: student team and advisor. Type: current state. Updated: 2026-09-08.

The user requested consideration of Mach 1 and other impressive achievements. This document owns the candidate goals. They are discussion targets, not approved flight requirements or demonstrated capabilities.

## Five candidate achievements

| ID | Tentative goal | Why it is compelling | Evidence of success | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Recover and re-fly the same primary airframe on three authorized flights | Demonstrates repeatability and repair discipline | Flight records, recovered data, inspections, replacement-part ledger | Proposed core mission; three flights is a planning target, not a reliability certification |
| A2 | Produce a synchronized flight replay using measured telemetry and video | Makes the invisible physics understandable to an audience | Labeled measurements, time alignment, units, uncertainty, and explicit data gaps | Proposed early software and instrumentation result |
| A3 | Demonstrate deployable wings on an inert article, then independently validate a glider | Preserves the visual transformation in the original concept | Recorded bench behavior and separately reviewed aircraft flight evidence | Proposed research track; successful bench deployment does not qualify a flight mechanism |
| A4 | Predict a held-out flight with a documented uncertainty envelope | Shows that the team understands its vehicle, rather than merely showing a successful flight | Predictions frozen before the test; measurements compared afterward; discrepancies explained | Proposed analysis result; acceptance tolerances set before testing |
| A5 | Investigate a verified Mach 1 crossing on a conventional, mentor-reviewed rocket | Provides a recognizable performance milestone | Credible measured evidence with uncertainty, valid interpretation, intact recovery, and range review | User-suggested stretch goal; feasibility for this team and vehicle is not established |

Recommended project identity: recoverable flight with visible mechanisms and convincing data. A1 and A2 make a coherent baseline. A3 supplies the original visual signature. A4 strengthens the engineering. A5 remains an optional separate branch.

## Mach 1: what is and is not established

Mach 1 means flight at the local speed of sound. A single fixed ground-speed threshold is not sufficient evidence of a crossing. The measurement method and atmospheric assumptions need review.

Near the speed of sound, shocks and wave drag can change aerodynamic behavior. A model validated only in lower-speed conditions cannot automatically support a transonic claim. [NASA drag overview](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/what-is-drag/)

NAR identifies supersonic-capable rockets as high-performance vehicles with specialized safety considerations. The fact that this category exists does not establish that the team's original large modular concept is suitable. [NAR safety officer guidance](https://www.nar.org/content.aspx?club_id=114127&module_id=673715&page_id=22)

Proposed separation of scope: study the speed goal on a conventional rocket track. Keep folding-wing development and glider testing independent until their own evidence supports further review. Do not combine first-time high-speed flight, novel separation, and aircraft transition into one initial test.

This repository does not select a motor, optimize geometry for a speed target, or provide powered guidance implementation. The first Mach 1 deliverable would be a feasibility and measurement review with the qualified team, budget, and range.

## Making the showcase memorable

| Exhibit | What the audience sees | Truth label |
|---|---|---|
| Original-size inert display | The full silhouette, removable panels, and wing mechanism | Concept / inert demonstrator |
| Recovered flight article | Real hardware with flight history and inspection photographs | Flown configuration and date |
| Telemetry replay | Measured flight alongside synchronized video and annotated events | Recorded data; interpolation and gaps identified |
| Model comparison | Prediction envelope beside the actual flight | Simulation versus measurement |
| Mechanism failure demonstration | On an inert fixture, explain what happens when expected movement fails | Bench test within approved fixture scope |

These are presentation elements for the five goals, not additional mandatory work tracks. A turbine engine is not needed to obtain this visual impact.

## Choosing the next goal

At G0, select one core achievement and at most one optional extension. Compare cost, available reviewers, test access, recoverability, and evidence quality. Do not rank options by appearance alone.

No Mach 2 or higher target is selected. More speed is not a required progression after Mach 1. Better repeatability, recovery, and prediction are valid later achievements.
